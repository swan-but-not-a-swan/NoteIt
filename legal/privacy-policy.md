## The short version

- **Your notes stay on your phone.** Photos, videos, notes, folders, tags and
  snippets are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive
  them, and we cannot see them.
- **The free version shows ads** served by Google AdMob, which collects some
  device information to show and measure those ads.
- **NoteIt Plus is sold through Apple or Google.** We never see your payment
  details. We use RevenueCat to know whether your subscription is active.

## 1. Information that stays on your device

NoteIt stores the following on your device only:

- Notes you write, and the photos and videos you capture or attach
- Folder names, colours and cover images
- Tags and snippets
- App settings, such as light or dark theme

This information is not sent to us. If you delete the app, it is deleted with
it. It may be included in your device's own backups (for example iCloud or
Google backup), depending on your device settings; those backups are governed
by Apple or Google, not by us.

### Camera and photo library

NoteIt asks for access to your camera and photo library only so you can create
and attach picture-notes. Media you choose stays on your device.

## 2. Advertising (free version)

The free version of NoteIt shows ads provided by **Google AdMob**. To show,
limit and measure ads, Google may collect:

- Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID, or on iOS the
  Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) only if you allow tracking
- IP address and approximate location derived from it
- Device type, operating system and app information
- Information about ads shown and your interactions with them

**Your choices**

- If you are in a region that requires consent (such as the EEA, the UK or
  Switzerland), we ask for your consent through Google's consent tool before
  ads are personalised.
- On iOS, we ask for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency
  prompt. You can change this later in **Settings > Privacy & Security >
  Tracking**.
- On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's
  **Settings > Privacy > Ads** (wording varies by device).
- Subscribers to NoteIt Plus do not see ads, and ads are not loaded for them.

Ads in NoteIt are limited to content rated for general audiences.

Learn how Google uses this information:
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## 3. NoteIt Plus subscriptions

NoteIt Plus is purchased through the **Apple App Store** or **Google Play**.
Apple or Google processes your payment. We never receive your card or bank
details.

We use **RevenueCat** to check whether your subscription or free trial is
active. RevenueCat receives:

- A random, anonymous app user ID created on your device (not your name or
  email)
- Purchase, trial and subscription status, and store transaction information
- Basic device and app information, such as operating system, app version and
  store country

RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## 4. What we do not do

- We do not sell your personal information.
- We do not require an account, name or email address to use NoteIt.
- We do not read, upload or analyse your notes, photos or videos.

## 5. Children

NoteIt is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from them.

## 6. Keeping and deleting information

- **On your device:** delete individual notes or folders in the app, or
  uninstall the app to remove everything.
- **Advertising data:** held by Google under its own retention policies. Use
  the device controls above to reset or delete your advertising identifier.
- **Subscription data:** held by RevenueCat and by Apple or Google. Contact us
  at [CONTACT EMAIL] if you would like us to request deletion of the
  subscription records linked to your app user ID.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or
restrict the use of your personal information, and to object to its
processing. Because NoteIt keeps your content on your device, most of it is
already under your direct control. For anything else, contact us at
[CONTACT EMAIL].
